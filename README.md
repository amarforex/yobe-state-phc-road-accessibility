Spatial Accessibility of Primary Health Centres to Roads in Yobe State
Project Overview
This project investigates the spatial accessibility of Primary Health Centres (PHCs) in Yobe State, Nigeria.
The project will identify Primary Health Centres that are located more than 10 km from an all-season road. The analysis will use spatial data on health facilities, roads, and administrative boundaries to identify potential areas where physical road access to primary healthcare facilities may be limited.
Spatial Question
Which primary health centres in Yobe State are located more than 10 km from an all-season road?
Study Area
The study area is Yobe State, Nigeria, including its Local Government Areas (LGAs).
The analysis will focus specifically on Primary Health Centres within Yobe State and their proximity to the available road network.
Why This Question?
Access to primary healthcare depends not only on the availability of health facilities but also on physical accessibility.
Mapping the distance between PHCs and roads can help identify facilities that may have weaker physical connectivity and can provide a useful starting point for understanding healthcare accessibility in Yobe State.
Datasets
1. Primary Health Centres
The project will use information on Primary Health Centres in Yobe State, including facility names, locations, and geographic coordinates.
Source: Nigeria National Health Facility Registry
Source link:
https://hfr.fmohconnect.gov.ng/
2. Road Network
OpenStreetMap road data will be used to represent the road network in Yobe State and assess the proximity of Primary Health Centres to roads.
Source: OpenStreetMap
Source link:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/
3. Yobe State and Local Government Boundaries
Administrative boundary data will be used to define the study area and identify the Local Government Areas within Yobe State.
Source: Humanitarian Data Exchange / OCHA Nigeria
Source link:
https://data.humdata.org/dataset/nga-administrative-boundaries
Planned Analysis
The project will:
1.	Obtain the required spatial datasets.
2.	Identify Primary Health Centres located within Yobe State.
3.	Obtain and prepare the road network for the study area.
4.	Obtain the Yobe State and LGA boundaries.
5.	Check and prepare the coordinate reference systems of the datasets.
6.	Calculate the distance between each PHC and the nearest relevant road.
7.	Identify PHCs located more than 10 km from an all-season road.
8.	Map the identified facilities.
9.	Summarise the results by Local Government Area where appropriate.
Expected Output
The main output will be a map showing Primary Health Centres and highlighting facilities located more than 10 km from an all-season road.
The project will also produce a simple summary of the identified facilities and their Local Government Areas.
Project Scope
This is a spatial accessibility screening exercise. Being more than 10 km from a mapped road does not by itself mean that a facility is inaccessible to patients. The result will therefore be treated as an indication for further investigation rather than a complete measure of healthcare accessibility.
