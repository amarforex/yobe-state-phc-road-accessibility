Week 01 Project Brief
GeoDev Lab
Project Title
Spatial Accessibility of Primary Health Centres to Roads in Yobe State, Nigeria

1. Spatial Question
Which primary health centres in Yobe State are located more than 10 km from an all-season road?
This question will be answered using spatial analysis of Primary Health Centre locations and the road network within Yobe State.

2. Study Area
Yobe State, Nigeria
The analysis will cover Primary Health Centres located within Yobe State and will use Local Government Areas as an administrative reference for summarising the results.

3. Datasets Required
Dataset 1: Primary Health Centres
What it contains:
Locations of health facilities in Yobe State, including Primary Health Centres and geographic coordinates.
Source: Nigeria National Health Facility Registry
Source link:
https://hfr.fmohconnect.gov.ng/

Dataset 2: Road Network
What it contains:
Road and transport network information required to assess the proximity of Primary Health Centres to roads.
Source: OpenStreetMap
Source link:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/

Dataset 3: Administrative Boundaries
What it contains:
Nigeria administrative boundaries, including State and Local Government Area boundaries. The data will be used to define Yobe State and its LGAs.
Source: Humanitarian Data Exchange / OCHA Nigeria
Source link:
https://data.humdata.org/dataset/nga-administrative-boundaries

4. Planned Spatial Analysis
The project will use the following general workflow:
1.	Obtain the Primary Health Centre dataset.
2.	Obtain the road network for Yobe State.
3.	Obtain the administrative boundaries for Yobe State and its LGAs.
4.	Filter the datasets to the Yobe State study area.
5.	Check and standardise the coordinate reference systems.
6.	Calculate the distance from each Primary Health Centre to the nearest relevant road.
7.	Identify facilities where the distance is greater than 10 km.
8.	Map the identified Primary Health Centres.
9.	Summarise the results by Local Government Area.

5. Expected Output
The primary output will be a spatial map showing:
•	Primary Health Centre locations;
•	the road network;
•	facilities located more than 10 km from an all-season road; and
•	the Local Government Areas in Yobe State.
A table summarising the identified facilities and their LGAs will also be produced.

6. Scope and Limitation
The project is intended as a spatial screening exercise.
More than 10 km from a mapped road should not automatically be interpreted as complete physical inaccessibility. The analysis will identify locations that may warrant further investigation.
The accuracy of the results will depend on the completeness, positional accuracy, and currency of the health-facility and road datasets.
